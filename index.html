How much will my cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on your home's size, the service and the condition of the space. Send your details so we can discuss a personalized quote before you book.

What's the difference between home and deep cleaning?

Home cleaning focuses on routine upkeep. Deep cleaning gives additional attention to buildup and details. We'll agree on the specific tasks before the visit.

Can I request carpet cleaning on its own?

Yes. Choose Carpet Cleaning and tell us the number of rooms or rugs, the material if known, and any spots you want us to assess. Results depend on the material and condition.

Does submitting the form confirm my appointment?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp message. Once you send it, we'll discuss availability, scope and price with you before confirming an appointment.

Do you serve my town in Connecticut?

Include your town or ZIP code in your request, and we'll confirm coverage and scheduling directly with you.
